The thread through every week

One app, built from scratch to production

CloudEats is a fictional food delivery platform — but every service, database, pipeline, and security rule you build in the labs is real and runs on your machine.

Weeks 1–2
First container
Docker environment, static frontend served from a container, VM networking basics
Weeks 3–4
Data layer

Week 5
Split into services

Lock it down
JWT authentication, role-based access control, secrets management
Weeks 9–10
Watch it run

Three tracks, one course

Learn at the speed that suits you

Every lecture and lab has the same content across three pace levels. Switch between them any time — no separate courses to manage.

🌱
Beginner

New to cloud or coming from a non-technical background. You need every step spelled out — and that's fine.

  • Every step is expanded on page load
  • Plain-English definitions for every term
  • ✅ Checkpoint after each section
  • Inline error guides next to the step that causes them
⚡
Average

You've used a terminal before and can follow along without hand-holding. You want to understand the why, not just the what.

  • Steps collapsed — expand what you need
  • "Why are we doing this?" callouts on key decisions
  • Checkpoint question at the end of each Part
  • Official docs linked where relevant
🚀
Advanced

Comfortable with Docker and Linux. You want trade-off discussions, extension challenges, and production-grade context.

  • All steps collapsed — scan headers, skip what you know
  • Challenge extension at the end of every lab
  • "What breaks if you change X?" prompts
  • Links to production-equivalent tooling
